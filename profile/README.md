## 🧠 Overview

**Gizmo Pro** is a Blender add-on focused on improving the way artists interact with transformation controls during modeling and object manipulation. It introduces alternative gizmo designs and interaction methods intended to make moving, rotating, and scaling geometry more direct and adaptable to different working styles.

The add-on is designed to accommodate both mouse-based and graphics-tablet workflows. Its transform controls can provide a more tactile approach to manipulating objects and mesh elements, while additional shortcuts and modifier-based interactions give users greater control over precise adjustments.

A selection of configurable gizmo layouts provides different visual approaches to transformation. Users can work with specialized manipulators, orientation controls, snapping options, and quick-access functions without constantly moving away from the main modeling area.

**Gizmo Pro** also includes workflow-oriented utilities that complement Blender's standard transformation tools. The combination of customizable manipulators, precision controls, and quick modeling functions can be useful for hard-surface work, general mesh editing, scene layout, and everyday object positioning.
